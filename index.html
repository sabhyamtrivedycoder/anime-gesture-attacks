📜 JUTSU & ATTACK GESTURE GUIDE

🐉 Kamehameha (かめはめ波)
🤲 Cup hands at hip ➔ Thrust forward
Hold both hands close together cupped to charge the cyan orb. Once charged, push your hands forward toward the screen to fire the beam!
🐉 Spirit Bomb (元気玉)
🙌 Raise both hands high above head
Raise both hands up towards the sky to gather energy from the universe. Once the celestial sphere is ready, bring hands down to drop it!
🐉 Super Saiyan Aura (超サイヤ人)
✊✊ Clench both hands into fists at chest/waist
Hold both fists tight to ignite roaring golden flames, bio-electric SSJ2 sparks, and vibrating screen tremors.
🍃 Rasengan (螺旋丸)
✋ Single open palm facing forward/up
Hold your palm open like you're holding a sphere. Dense spinning chakra vortex rings will swirl rapidly in your palm.
🍃 Chidori (千鳥)
👇 Hand held pointing downward with clawed fingers
Bend fingers claw-like pointing down. Unbridled electrical lightning arcs violently crackle with the sound of 1,000 birds!
🍃 Shadow Clone Jutsu (影分身の術)
🤞 Cross your two index fingers into a '+' seal
Extend both index fingers and intersect them into a cross seal. Giant anime smoke puffs erupt with classic ninja sound!
🍃 Amaterasu (天照)
✌️ Two-finger sword seal pointing forward
Extend index and middle fingers together (gun/sword seal) and point. Black flames and Mangekyo Sharingan ignite where you aim.
🤞 Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void (無量空処)
🤞 Gojo Satoru Hand Sign (crossed index/middle)
Form Gojo's signature mudra with index and middle finger pointing up. Reality shatters into a deep space black hole cosmic void!
🤞 Hollow Purple (虚式「紫」)
🔴 Left open + 🔵 Right open ➔ Clasp hands together
Hold both hands open apart to summon Reversal: Red and Convergence: Blue. Bring hands together to fuse them into an all-erasing Purple Singularity!
🤞 Malevolent Shrine (伏魔御厨子: 解・捌)
🙏 Sukuna Prayer Mudra / Clasp both hands flat
Clasp hands together flat in Sukuna's Enmaten prayer sign. Blood-red domain aura and razor-sharp Dismantle blade slashes slice the screen!
🤞 Black Flash (黒閃)
👊 Fast punch / fist thrust towards camera
Throw a rapid forward punch strike toward the camera. Space distorts within 0.000001 seconds followed by explosive jet-black lightning!
