# ⚡ Anime Gesture Attacks (DBZ, Naruto, Jujutsu Kaisen)

Perform iconic anime attacks in front of your webcam using real-time hand gesture recognition, dynamic canvas particle effects, screen shake, manga calligraphy banners, and procedural anime sound synthesis (Web Audio API)!

---

## 🌟 Supported Anime & Attacks

### 🐉 Dragon Ball Z (DBZ)
1. **Kamehameha (かめはめ波)**:
   - **Gesture**: Cup both hands together at hip or chest level to charge the glowing blue energy sphere. Once the energy gauge fills, thrust both palms forward towards the screen to unleash a massive incandescent beam!
2. **Spirit Bomb / Genki Dama (元気玉)**:
   - **Gesture**: Raise both hands high into the air with open palms. Energy motes stream from the edges into a giant celestial orb. Lower your hands quickly to drop the bomb and trigger a supernova shockwave.
3. **Super Saiyan Aura / Power Up (超サイヤ人)**:
   - **Gesture**: Clench both hands into tight fists at your sides. Roaring golden flame aura, bio-electric SSJ2 lightning sparks, and vibrating screen tremors ignite around you!

### 🍃 Naruto
4. **Rasengan (螺旋丸)**:
   - **Gesture**: Hold a single hand open with palm facing forward/upwards as if palming a sphere. High-velocity rotating chakra vortex rings swirl rapidly in your palm.
5. **Chidori (千鳥 - One Thousand Birds)**:
   - **Gesture**: Hold your hand pointing downwards with fingers curled claw-like. Violent jagged lightning bolts erupt with the high-pitched chirping screech of 1,000 birds!
6. **Shadow Clone Jutsu (影分身の術 - Kage Bunshin no Jutsu)**:
   - **Gesture**: Intersect both your index fingers into a `+` cross seal. A giant anime white smoke puff explosion bursts with classic ninja jutsu audio!
7. **Amaterasu (天照)**:
   - **Gesture**: Extend your index and middle fingers together into a two-finger sword seal (or gun gesture) and point. Jet-black inextinguishable flames erupt with Mangekyo Sharingan target lock!

### 🤞 Jujutsu Kaisen (JJK)
8. **Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void / Muryōkūsho (領域展開: 無量空処 - Gojo Satoru)**:
   - **Gesture**: Form Gojo Satoru's signature hand mudra (crossed index and middle fingers pointing upright). Reality shatters into a deep space domain with cosmic accretion disk, black hole event horizon, and infinite information overload!
9. **Hollow Purple (虚式「紫」 - Kyoshiki: Murasaki)**:
   - **Gesture**: Hold left hand open (Reversal: Red) and right hand open (Convergence: Blue). Bring both hands together into a clasp or finger gun pointing forward. Red and Blue collide into an all-erasing Purple Singularity beam!
10. **Malevolent Shrine / Dismantle & Cleave (伏魔御厨子: 解・捌 - Ryomen Sukuna)**:
    - **Gesture**: Clasp hands flat in Sukuna's Enmaten prayer seal. Blood-red domain aura and razor-sharp slicing blade slashes crisscross the screen!
11. **Black Flash (黒閃 - Kokusen)**:
    - **Gesture**: Rapid forward punch strike toward the camera with a closed fist. Space distorts within 0.000001 seconds followed by an explosive blast of jet-black and crimson lightning!

## 🚀 How to Run

### Method 1: Using the provided `start.bat`
Double-click `start.bat` in this folder. It will launch the local Python server and automatically open the application in your default browser.

### Method 2: Command Line
Run the following in PowerShell:
```powershell
& "C:\Users\PC\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python312\python.exe" server.py
```
Then open `http://localhost:8000` in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any modern web browser.

## 🎮 Features
- **Zero Audio Assets Required**: Uses the Web Audio API to procedurally synthesize authentic sci-fi anime sounds, energy hums, electrical crackles, explosions, and deep sub-bass!
- **Voice Announcements**: Optional Web Speech API anime shouts ("KAMEHAMEHA!", "RYOIKI TENKAI!", "RASENGAN!").
- **Camera Mirroring**: Toggle mirror mode so movements feel intuitive.
- **Snapshot Capture**: Click the **Capture** button to download a high-res photo combining your camera pose and the active anime VFX!
- **Quick Test Dock**: Click any attack card at the bottom to test its sound effects and visual animations instantly even without a camera.
- **Custom Universe Filter**: Filter between All Anime, DBZ, Naruto, or Jujutsu Kaisen.
